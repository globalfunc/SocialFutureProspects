The row lists `country: "USA (Florida)"`, `tzDiffHours: -7`, and `localTimeInSlot: "05:00-08:00 EDT

Checked directly: Rising Tide's confirmed locations (Parkland, Margate, Coral Springs, and the

Florida**, which is genuinely in the U.S. Eastern time zone — the same real zone as Kentucky. No
source found today suggests either D'Eri has relocated. **This means the identical fields are a
genuine coincidence of both prospects sitting in the same real time zone, not a copy-paste data
error** — the same conclusion reached for Amar Latif's, Elin Williams's, and Holly Tuke's UK rows
sharing a time zone with each other.

On 29 September 2026, Florida is on **Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4)** and Bulgaria is on
**Eastern European Summer Time (EEST, UTC+3)** — a 7-hour difference, matching `tzDiffHours: -7`
exactly. The event slot (12:00 PM–3:00 PM EEST) converts to **5:00 AM–8:00 AM EDT** — this arithmetic
was double-checked the same way it was for Sam Seavey and Holly Tuke, and is correct, matching the
row's own `localTimeInSlot` exactly. No correction needed.
