## Contact / addressing decision — verified today, not assumed from the row

The row's `contact` field lists three possible routes: `traveleyes-international.com`, his personal
site `amarlatif.com`, or a speaker bureau (Speakers Associates / Leading Authorities / Gordon
Poole). All three were checked today rather than picking the first one listed:

1. **Traveleyes' own contact page** (`traveleyes-international.com/contact-us/`, fetched
   2026-09-18) is a **general customer-service page** — phone numbers, a booking-enquiry form, and
   postal addresses for holiday bookings, plus a separate `media@traveleyes-international.com`
   address for press. Nothing on it routes to Amar personally or mentions speaking engagements.
   **Ruled out** — this would land in a booking/commercial inbox, not with him.
2. **His personal site's contact page** (`amarlatif.com/contact`, fetched 2026-09-18) has **two
   distinct, clearly labelled routes**: a form captioned "For all public speaking enquiries please
   fill out the form below and a member of the team will be in touch," and, separately, a named
   contact at Arlington Talent (Annie Sweetbaum) explicitly for **television** enquiries. The
   public-speaking form is exactly the category this invitation falls into, and it is the most
   direct route that is actually about him rather than the company he founded.
3. **Speaker bureaus** (Speakers Associates, Leading Authorities, Gordon Poole, plus JLA, London
   Speaker Bureau, Specialist Speakers, and Evolve Speakers Agency all turned up representing him)
   confirm he is professionally represented and speaks commercially to corporate boards — Speakers
   Associates' own bio for him states "his corporate CV gives him credibility with a board... he
   makes the commercial case for inclusion in the language a board expects to hear it in," and lists
   paying clients like Goldman Sachs and Barclays Capital. **Ruled out for this invitation**: bureaus
   exist to book and invoice paid corporate keynotes, and a foreign non-profit's unpaid speaking
   request is a poor fit for that channel — it risks either a fee quote that stalls the invitation or
   being deprioritized behind paying enquiries.

**Recommendation, matching the routing already specified in the task: send via the public-speaking
enquiry form at `amarlatif.com/contact`.** This is confirmed today to be the correct, live, and
best-suited route for an unpaid speaking invitation — more direct than Traveleyes' commercial
contact page, and a better fit than a paid-engagement bureau. Since it is a web form rather than an
email inbox, paste the subject line as the first line of the message field (or into a subject field
if the form has one) so whoever screens it can identify the invitation before reading the full body.
Send the LinkedIn message in parallel to his own profile as a direct, informal flag that the formal
invitation is on its way.
