## Timezone and location — verified from current research, not assumed from the row

The row lists `country: "USA (Nicholasville, KY)"` and `tzDiffHours: -7`. No source found today
suggests a relocation — every profile checked, old and new, keeps him in the Lexington, Kentucky
area (his employer, the Bluegrass Council of the Blind, is itself headquartered in Lexington proper,
a short distance from Nicholasville, which likely explains why several third-party bios say "lives in
Lexington" while his own business's current mailing address is in Nicholasville — treated as the same
local area, not a contradiction).

Kentucky is entirely within the Eastern time zone. On 29 September 2026, Kentucky is on **Eastern
Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4)** and Bulgaria is on **Eastern European Summer Time (EEST, UTC+3)** — a
7-hour difference, matching the row's `tzDiffHours: -7` exactly. The event slot (12:00 PM–3:00 PM
EEST) converts to **5:00 AM–8:00 AM EDT** — this arithmetic was double-checked and is correct,
matching the row's own `localTimeInSlot` ("05:00-08:00 EDT (early but workable)") exactly. No
correction is needed here, unlike Holly Tuke's row.
